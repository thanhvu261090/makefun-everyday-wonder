One sentence on a small moment of wonder in everyday life: a shadow that seems to know the way home better than the person casting it.

> "All the way home my shadow on the wet pavement walked half a step ahead of me, as if it already knew the way and was in no hurry to arrive."

## How it works

`main.py` holds the entry sentence as a constant, checks it against the kit's gate (UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace, a single sentence on one line with no padding), and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` when run with `python3 main.py`.
